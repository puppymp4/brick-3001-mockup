Why 3001?

Look closer at our logo. It's one brick drawn three ways: from the side, from above and from below. That brick is part 3001, the 2x4.

3001
LEGO® design numbers start here. The first number in the series went to the 2x4 brick.
1:58 pm
On January 28, 1958, the LEGO Group filed the patent for the stud-and-tube brick in Copenhagen.
3 tubes
Underneath, the tubes grip the studs of the brick below. The LEGO Group calls it clutch power.
915,103,765
Ways to put six 2x4 bricks together.

Flip a 2x4 over and you'll usually find 3001 molded right on the bottom. The patent drawings for the brick and the minifigure hang framed by our build counter. We named the shop after the brick everything else stands on.

Common questions

Are you the official LEGO® Store?

No. Brick 3001 is an independent shop owned by two brothers. We aren't affiliated with the LEGO Group. We just love the bricks.

Do you buy used LEGO® bricks?

Yes. Loose bulk is what we want most, more than already-built sets. Email or DM us a photo, or just bring it in.

Can I build in the store?

Yes. Bring a set from home or buy one here, then grab a stool at the build counter.

Do you sell individual parts?

Yes. At the sorting tables a small box is $10 and a big box is $18. Single parts are priced against BrickLink.

When are you open?

Monday 10 AM to 4 PM, Thursday through Saturday 10 AM to 9 PM, and Sunday 10 AM to 6 PM. Closed Tuesday and Wednesday.
